About

Jaemin Cho is a Unity and C# game developer and pixel artist studying Computer Game Design at George Mason University, with graduation expected in 2027. Jaemin has shipped more than ten playable games in Unity and Unreal Engine, from solo game jam entries to club projects with up to 14 teammates. The solo horror game Shh placed first overall at the Mason Korea Game Jam Fall 2025, and Save the Pig, an educational game co-programmed with the Gnonymous club for FAO Korea, was exhibited at the World Food Forum 2023 in Rome. Beyond programming gameplay and UI, Jaemin has served as Vice President of Gnonymous and has taught Unity and C# as a lecturer and Learning Assistant. Currently, Jaemin is building the mobile platformer Cat'astrophe with a team of six and a chess game developed through AI pair programming to grow as a developer. Jaemin is committed to finishing and shipping games, and works best in collaborative teams.
